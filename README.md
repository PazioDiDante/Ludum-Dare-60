# Build, repair, automate

Give old electronics a second life in your own little workshop!

Take gadgets apart and collect the components inside. Fit them onto a circuit board, connect them with wires, and balance their stats to complete orders. You'll need enough power, manageable heat, and a layout that actually fits.

Complete orders and solder boards to earn coins, then spend them on more gadgets, upgrades, and robots. Recycle spare parts into scrap and craft the components you're missing.

Once you have robots helping with dismantling, soldering, and crafting, keep their generator running—and try not to set it on fire.

*Created for the **Ludum Dare 60** game jam. Made with **GameMaker**.*
